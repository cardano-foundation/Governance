## Summary

The meeting focused on key updates including the expiration of the Delta DeFi proposal. Significant attention was given to the critically low Stake Pool Operator (SPO) participation in ongoing parameter change action.

## Details

- **Governance Action Status Updates:** Delta DeFi Proposal expired on January 29th and failed to achieve DRep approval with only 11.9% YES votes. Net Change Limit (NCL) governance action has successfully reached the required DRep threshold of 50%.
- **Stake Pool Operator (SPO) Participation:** Participation in the current governance action  Parameter Change for Memory Units increase remains critically low. It was observed that SPOs are reacting slowly to the governance requirements, with many pools expressing uncertainty regarding the voting process. To encourage participation, the CF DRep and Stake Pools will record their votes as soon as possible. This will be followed by a communication push emphasizing the current lack of threshold achievement to highlight the urgency for pool operators to engage. SPO Round Table Talk planning was initiated and outreach initiatives are ongoing.
