## Summary

The meeting addressed the procedural ambiguity regarding a perceived Constitutional Committee (CC) voting requirement on Net Change Limits (NCL). Consensus was reached that there is alignment in CC voting rationales that the NCL is approved and active. Discussions were held on the 2 parameter change actions and current approval rates.

## Details

- **Net Change Limit (NCL) Oversight and Strategy:** A key discussion focused on whether the Constitution requires a full Constitutional Committee (CC) vote for NCL proposals. It was determined that, as the NCL is an off-chain construct, the CC's primary function is to formally interpret and classify the proposal as a valid NCL. This classification is intended to provide necessary clarity for DReps during the subsequent voting process, rather than the CC itself approving the NCL. Consensus was noted among CC members' voting rationales and media posts (including those CC members who failed to vote on the NCL governance action) that the current NCL is valid without requiring a full on-chain CC vote. Consequently, an active NCL (350M ada) will take effect at the beginning of epoch 612 on the 9 February 2026\.
- **Memory Units Parameter Change:** The team discussed the voting outcomes for the proposal to increase transaction and block memory units. A substantial effort was required to mobilize voting participation among the Stake Pool Operators (SPOs) and DReps, resulting in the proposal meeting its necessary thresholds just before the expiry date on the 13 February 2026\.
- **CC Member Inactivity and Minimum Size Requirement:** The team discussed the recent community concerns and the governance action submitted in response to the CC member count temporarily dropping below the required minimum and the ongoing issue of inactivity of some members. This included a review of the current governance framework's provisions for the removal and replacement of inactive CC members and contemplation of the possible paths to address the issue beyond changing on-chain parameters.
