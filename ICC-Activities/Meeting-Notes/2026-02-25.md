## Summary

The meeting centered on the status of the upcoming budget process info action which is expected to be submitted on-chain shortly. The team reviewed several expired governance actions, noting the constitutional failure of the DeFi Liquidity Budget and technical issues leading to the early expiration of the parameter change to lower the Constitutional Committee (CC) minimum size. Current deliberations focused on the 300 million ada Net Change Limit (NCL) and the extensive Amaru Treasury withdrawal proposal.

## Details

- **Budget Cycle On-Chain Transition and Coordination:** The upcoming Intersect budget cycle process is expected to be submitted as an info action for a vote. This action addresses the approval of the *process* itself rather than specific figures.
- **Budget Committee Representation and Governance:** Internal shifts within the budget committee were discussed, specifically regarding the transition of CF representation.
- **Review of Expired Actions and Technical Failures:** The team reviewed the expiration of the Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity budget, which was unanimously found to be unconstitutional and failed to meet the required DRep threshold for withdrawal. Additionally, the proposal to reduce the minimum CC size from seven to five expired prematurely due to a technical cancellation triggered by the approval of the previous parameter change to [increase the the transaction and block memory units](https://adastat.net/governances/c21b00f90f18fce4003edf42b0b0d455126e01c946e80cc5341a9f9750caf79500). Despite the technical failure, the team recorded a "yes" vote to signal support for the underlying intent.
- **Amaru Treasury Withdrawal:** The Amaru withdrawal proposal was presented, including detailed annexes, a retrospective of 2025 activities, and a defined scope for 2026\.
