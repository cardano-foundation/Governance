## Summary

The meeting focused on discussing live governance actions. The team reviewed the status of the 300 million ada Net Change Limit (NCL) and the Amaru Treasury withdrawal, identifying technical challenges and constitutional hurdles for the latter. Additionally, a timeline for the Intersect budget process was discussed, which is targeting July for the first treasury payouts. Coordination efforts are underway to ensure the budget process info action is submitted before the epoch boundary from 616 to 617 to avoid administrative vacuums.

## Details

- **Intersect Budget Process and Timeline:** The Intersect budget process info action is currently awaiting board approval and is expected to be submitted before the end of epoch 616\. The proposed timeline opens proposal submissions on the Ekklesia platform in April extending through early May. Following a two-week review and feedback period, an off-chain voting phase is scheduled for late May. The current objective is to submit two treasury withdrawals by mid-June, with the first approvals and payouts anticipated by the middle to end of July.
- **Net Change Limit (NCL) Status and Rationale:** The 300 million ada NCL info action is live until March 10th and currently sits at approximately 33% approval, which remains below the 50% threshold required for passage. The team notes that the 300 M ada figure aligns with last year’s inflows. The supporting rationale will also address concerns raised by some Constitutional Committee (CC) members regarding the validity of the previous 350 million NCL to ensure constitutional clarity for DReps.
- **Amaru Treasury Withdrawal and Constitutional Hurdles:** The Amaru withdrawal proposal remains live for three weeks, during which time a non-critical error was identified in a secondary PDF reference regarding an outdated ada withdrawal amount. Procedural challenges were discussed, specifically the impact of a CC member's abstention due to project-level interests, which reduces the voting body to six members.
- **Governance Coordination and Calendar Requirements:** The team identified a pressing need for a centralized calendar or list to track upcoming governance actions, including hard forks, parameter changes, and CC elections. A preference was discussed for Intersect to manage a public-facing calendar view, enhancing visibility for all stakeholders.
