## Summary

Key updates of the meeting included the state of the 300 million ada Net Change Limit (NCL) and the status of the Amaru Treasury withdrawal, which requires Constitutional Committee support. The team reviewed Intersect’s new 5-stage budget process, expressing technical concerns over the framing of the Cardano 2030 vision. Discussion also covered Dingo, a node implementation written in Go (Golang) and the resubmission of the DeFi Liquidity Stablecoin proposal.

## Details

- **Net Change Limit (NCL) Outcome and Governance Resilience:** The governance action proposing the 300 million ada Net Change Limit (NCL) failed to meet the required 50% approval threshold and has expired. Despite this outcome, it was noted as a positive development that the Eastern Cardano Council has become operationally active again and will resume participation in future voting cycles.
- **Amaru Treasury Withdrawal and Stakeholder Engagement:** On March 11, the Amaru withdrawal proposal maintained approximately 52% support among DReps. Achieving the constitutional threshold requires four additional "yes" votes from the Constitutional Committee (CC). Should the current action fail, a strategic contingency plan has been formulated by the Amaru team to split the proposal into core engineering and integration modules for resubmission.
- **Intersect Budget Process and Vision Alignment:** Intersect has submitted an info action detailing a new five-stage budget process utilizing the Ekklesia tool. While the process is intended to improve transparency and support teams with lower administrative capacity, the team identified concerns regarding the constraints of funding to the Cardano 2030 vision.
- **Technical Node Diversification (Dingo Implementation):** The team evaluated the Dingo treasury withdrawal request for 6.9 million ada to develop a Go-based node client. While the technical merit of creating a third node implementation for network fault tolerance was affirmed, concerns were raised regarding the transparency of the proposal. It was noted that Dingo targets enterprise-level cloud deployment, differentiating its market position from the lightweight, performance-focused objectives of the Amaru project.
- **Review of DeFi Liquidity and Strategic Resubmissions:** The DeFi Liquidity Stablecoin committee has resubmitted the first of its planned two treasury withdrawal proposals following a previous rejection on constitutional grounds. The Foundation is currently performing a comparative analysis of the new metadata and rationale against the previous submission to determine if the identified constitutional issues have been sufficiently mitigated. This review will dictate whether a full re-evaluation is required or if the previous approval remains applicable.
