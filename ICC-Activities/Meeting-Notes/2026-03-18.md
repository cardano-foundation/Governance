## Summary

The meeting focused on the finalization of the Amaru Treasury withdrawal, which has exceeded the 67% approval threshold and is scheduled for enactment on March 25th. The team established a strategic voting position for the Draper Dragon Orion Fund and the Project Catalyst Managing Entity proposal. Additionally, the team addressed the current status of the Net Change Limit (NCL) and the Intersect Budget Process Framework.

## Details

- **Amaru Enactment and Net Change Limit (NCL) Status:** The Amaru treasury withdrawal proposal has successfully cleared the 67% DRep approval threshold, as of  March 18th standing at approximately 68.4%. Enactment is expected to occur on March 25th. In parallel, the status of the 350 million ada Net Change Limit (NCL) was reviewed; with four of the previously missing Constitutional Committee (CC) votes now cast, the limit is considered operationally active. However, it was noted that internal disagreements persist within the CC regarding the interpretative validity of the NCL approval process. There is a recommendation that such fundamental disagreements on constitutional interpretation should be resolved through formal constitutional amendments to ensure long-term procedural clarity.
- **Strategic Voting Position on Ecosystem Proposals:** The team opted to abstain from the voting to appoint the Foundation as the Project Catalyst managing entity until the community had a chance to state their opinion.
- **Evaluation of Active Governance Frameworks and Node Diversity:** The Intersect Budget Process Framework currently sits at 44% approval, nearing its 51% target. The team affirmed the value of this framework in reducing the administrative burden for smaller development teams, though it was observed that larger proposals continue to operate outside this structure. Regarding node diversity, the Dingo node implementation proposal remains at a low 9% approval level at the time of the meeting. While the technical merits of a Go-based client for network fault tolerance were recognized, the proposal’s timing and perceived overlap with the Amaru project were identified as primary factors for its current lack of community traction.
- **Resubmission of Treasury Withdrawals and Economic Adjustments:** The DeFi Liquidity budget withdrawal proposal was reviewed following its resubmission. The requested amount has been adjusted upward from 500,000 ada to 800,000 ada to mitigate the impact of market volatility on the establishment of legal structures and smart contract audits. The updated Governance Action metadata now includes explicit provisions for the return of unspent funds to the treasury, aligning with the requirements of the new Constitution. The proposal currently holds 23% approval, which the team views as a positive signal of improving community confidence in the revised technical and legal framework.
