## Summary

The meeting focused on the ratification of the Amaru Treasury withdrawal and the commencement of the Intersect budget process, which has officially met its approval threshold. The team conducted a strategic review of several active governance actions, including the Dingo Node implementation and the Draper Dragon Orion Fund, balancing technical redundancy needs against fiscal and performance concerns.

## Details

- **Amaru and Intersect Governance Milestone Status:** The Amaru treasury withdrawal has reached its approval threshold and is scheduled for enactment at the epoch boundary on March 25th. This milestone ensures that funding will begin transitioning into the defined smart contracts for core engineering activities. Simultaneously, the Intersect budget process framework has surpassed its 50% approval objective, allowing for the proposal submission phase to kick-off in the first half of April. The established timeline includes proposal collection from April 13th through early May, followed by a review cycle and an off-chain polling phase. This process utilizes a 67% approval threshold for participating stakes to determine which proposals proceed to the final on-chain treasury withdrawals scheduled for late June.
- **Technical Node Redundancy (Dingo Node):** A strategic evaluation of the Dingo node proposal emphasized the technical value of establishing a third professionally maintained node client to ensure network redundancy. It was argued that three node implementations offer a significantly higher degree of network security than two, particularly as the future maintenance requirements for existing implementations may vary. The proposal’s internal management costs remain a point of critique.
- **Treasury Withdrawal Oversight for Orion Fund:** The Draper Dragon Orion Fund is currently under review following concerns from prominent Cardano DReps regarding the lack of explicit KPIs and measurable milestones. It was clarified that the structured withdrawal system, where every tranche requires an individual treasury withdrawal governance action, allows DReps to retain granular control over the fund’s performance.
- **Strategic Abstention for Managing Entity Appointment:** The governance action requesting the appointment of the Foundation as the managing entity for Project Catalyst has reached approximately 50% community approval as of March 25th. In alignment with established governance ethics, the team reaffirmed its strategy to record a vote for this action. The team will wait until mid-April to cast this vote, monitoring community support levels to ensure the transition remains transparent and community-led.
