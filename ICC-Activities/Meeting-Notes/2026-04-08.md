## Summary

The meeting addressed the start of the new Intersect budget cycle preparation and the successful ratification of the Cardano Draper Orion Fund, which will see an initial outflow of 50 million ada. The team reviewed the status of several expiring governance actions, noting that the Dingo and DeFi Liquidity Budget proposals are currently below the required approval thresholds. Significant community support was observed for the Project Catalyst transition, with nearly 75% DRep approval. Finally, a technical evaluation was initiated for the Pebble and Gerolamo infrastructure proposal, focusing on the practical utility of the light-client and a TypeScript-based smart contract language relative to its 8 million ada cost.

## Details

- **Ratification and Enactment of the Orion Fund:** It was confirmed that the Cardano Draper Orion Fund has successfully cleared the required approval thresholds, securing over 70% DRep approval along with formal confirmation from the Constitutional Committee. The proposal is scheduled for ratification and enactment during the transition from epoch 623 to 624\. Consequently, the requested 50 million ada will flow out of the treasury in the first slots of epoch 624, marking an important milestone in ecosystem venture scaling and oversight.
- **Assessment of Expiring Governance Actions:** Two governance actions, the Dingo node implementation and the Cardano DeFi Liquidity Budget, are set to expire on April 9th. Current tracking indicates that both proposals are unlikely to meet the DRep approval thresholds. The Dingo proposal currently stands at 43% approval, significantly below the 67% requirement. The DeFi Liquidity Budget holds 51% approval; while closer to the threshold, its passage remains contingent on several large voting entities.
- **Project Catalyst Transition and Community Mandate:** The info governance action seeking approval for the Foundation to assume the role of managing entity for Project Catalyst has reached approximately 75% DRep approval. The action remains live until April 19th, and the team anticipates the approval percentage may increase further as additional voting power is recorded. This transition is viewed as a key step in stabilizing Catalyst administration and ensuring continuity for future funding rounds.
- **Technical Review of Pebble and Gerolamo Infrastructure:** A governance action proposing an 8 million ADA treasury withdrawal for Pebble (a TypeScript-based smart contract language) and Gerolamo (a Cardano light node) is currently under review. While the goal of creating browser-based light nodes aligns with decentralization objectives, the team raised technical questions regarding the practical use cases for developing smart contracts within a browser environment. Given the scale of the infrastructure investment, a formal technical outreach has been initiated to gather feedback from senior developers and engineers from within the Foundation to determine if the proposal’s technical merits justify the requested funding.
