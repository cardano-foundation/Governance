## Summary

The meeting addressed the successful ratification and enactment of the Dingo and Cardano DeFi Liquidity treasury withdrawals. Significant deliberations focused on the Pebble and Gerolamo proposal, with the team identifying technical risks regarding personnel allocations and the efficacy of bundled infrastructure. A strategic debate was held concerning the Cardano Summit/Token2049 treasury withdrawal.

## Details

- **Governance Action Expirations:** Two treasury withdrawals, the Dingo node implementation and the Cardano DeFi liquidity budget, have been formally ratified and enacted. Simultaneously, the info governance action for the Project Catalyst transition is in its final epoch with approximately 80% approval. This strong community consensus indicates support for the Foundation to assume management responsibilities. The team intends to record its final vote for the Catalyst entity before the end of the current epoch.
- **Pebble and Gerolamo Infrastructure Evaluation:** The combined proposal for the Pebble, a typescript smart contract programming language, and Gerolamo light node is currently live with 41% approval, remaining below the 67% requirement. Technical review raised concerns regarding the bundling of these two distinct projects, with some members finding Pebble more compelling than the light client. Further discussed was project’s internal staffing model, specifically the potential "double-counting" of full-time equivalents (FTEs) across multiple workstreams. There is a general consensus that if the proposal fails, the Foundation would support the resubmission of the projects as independent actions with more transparent personnel allocations and delivery roadmaps.
- **Strategic Utility of Browser-Based Light Clients:** A deep technical debate occurred regarding the value proposition of the Gerolamo light client. While it was argued that a light client could theoretically reduce infrastructure costs for builders, counter-arguments suggested that building costs on Cardano are already relatively low. The light client approach was characterized as serving a specific requirement for trustlessness and decentralization rather than addressing an immediate economic or simplicity bottleneck. The team emphasized that high-level infrastructure investments must clearly differentiate between addressing niche needs and solving widespread technical barriers for the broader developer ecosystem before receiving substantial treasury support.
- **Cardano Summit and TOKEN2049 Treasury Withdrawal:** To address community concerns regarding centralized influence, there is a strong internal recommendation for the Foundation’s representative to record an "abstain" vote. This action would serve as a signal to the community that the Foundation is not attempting to unduly influence the outcome and acknowledges the "power control" sensitivities within the DRep ecosystem. The objective is to ensure that the Foundation’s voting behavior does not inadvertently pressure other major stakeholders while maintaining transparency and adhering to established governance ethics.
