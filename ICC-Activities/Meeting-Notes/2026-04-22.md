## Summary

The meeting focused on the Cardano Summit and TOKEN2049 proposal following significant community opposition. Technical deliberations continued for the Pebble and Gerolamo governance action, specifically addressing the Constitutional Committee’s interpretation of unconstitutionality and the technical advantages of TypeScript-based frameworks for developer onboarding. Additionally, the team addressed claims regarding the Foundation's influence over delegated voting power, affirming a strict policy of non-interference.

## Details

- **Pebble and Gerolamo Governance Update and Technical Context:** The Pebble and Gerolamo governance action currently stands at 45% approval. Notably, the Eastern Cardano Council (ECC) has deemed the proposal "unconstitutional," citing undefined audit structure requirements. In ongoing deliberations, the team reviewed recent feedback from Subject Matter Experts (SMEs). To navigate the ECC's concerns, some suggested splitting the joint proposal into two separate treasury withdrawals. Despite these procedural roadblocks, the overarching consensus among SMEs and the advisory team is that Pebble remains a highly promising initiative.
- **Cardano Summit and Token 2049** The joint proposal for the Cardano Summit and Token 2049 is currently facing substantial opposition, with "no" votes exceeding 33%. It was discussed that CF plans to address Community concerns over an AMA and thereafter will assess the Community’s decision.
- **Clarification on DRep Delegation and Voting Autonomy:** The team addressed external claims regarding the voting behavior of DReps receiving delegations from the Foundation. It was explicitly clarified that there are no conditions or instructions provided to these DReps on how to cast their votes, only that DReps are active. The Foundation maintains a strict policy of non-interference, ensuring that delegated voting power remains independent and that all DReps are free to vote according to their own technical and strategic assessments. This autonomy is central to the Foundation's commitment to decentralized governance and the integrity of the DRep ecosystem, countering any suggestions that these delegates are required to align with the Foundation's own treasury withdrawal requests.
