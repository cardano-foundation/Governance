## Summary

The meeting addressed 9 active governance actions, prioritizing strategic alignment and technical evaluations of several high-budget proposals. The team discussed recommendations regarding the TWEAG protocol proposal and the OriLife x TonFarm agricultural traceability initiative, highlighting technical feasibility and structural concerns which require clarification. The Plutus Cost Model parameter change was assessed for expedited processing to address on-chain bugs, and the Eternl withdrawal was evaluated as a good example for treasury-supported sustainability. Discussions also focused on the Cardano Vision 2026 proposal and the preservation of public voting records.

## Details

- **TWEAG Protocol Restructuring and Re-evaluation Strategy:** The team evaluated the TWEAG proposal, which is currently raising concerns over its two-year timeline and broad scope. The primary proponents have indicated an intent to reset the voting round and resubmit a revised, 12-month proposal with a reduced budget, focused on deploying Peras to mainnet. The advisory team finalized its recommendation for the current submission, accompanied by a brief public rationale advising resubmission under the refined, narrower scope to ensure community alignment.
- **Agricultural Traceability and Commercial Funding Integrity:** A review of the agricultural traceability proposal for Asian fruit exports resulted in a recommendation regarding the current application. The team assessed the project's technical feasibility, noting that geolocation-based tree identification does not resolve post-harvest label fraud at the supply chain level, while the proposed requirement for farmers to purchase proprietary utility tokens presents an operational risk. Structurally, the team reaffirmed its position that the public treasury should not act as a subsidy for private business operations without clear equity upside or structured loan agreements, recommending that the project begin with a smaller, independent proof of concept.
- **Omnibus Research Proposal and Technical Unbundling:** Deliberations focused on the bundled research proposal submitted by IO. While recognizing the value of core workstreams like post-quantum security, technical advisors raised concerns regarding overlaps with previously funded consensus research and the omission of a mandatory project duration, which may render the action non-compliant with constitutional guidelines. The team favored a neutral stance to reinforce the strategic principle that omnibus proposals must be unbundled into separate, independent technical packages to ensure granular governance oversight.
- **Plutus Parameter Change:** The team reviewed the Plutus cost model parameter changes, which have undergone benchmark testing and validation across technical committees and testnet environments. The team agreed to recommend expedited passage of the proposal ahead of the standard six-week window.
- **Eternl Wallet and Sustainability Support:** The transition proposal for a prominent community wallet requesting a 1,680,000 ada loan was evaluated. The proposal aims to bridge the wallet's operations away from historical funding models toward a sustainable subscription and feature integration model, with repayment terms bound to future profitability thresholds. The team noted that the treasury is an appropriate vehicle to support stable community infrastructure through transitional phases, particularly as the proposed upgrades provide governance and transaction integrations.
- **Delegation Trust and Public Accountability Dynamics:** In response to reports of external pressure directed at delegated representatives, the team conducted a debate on the feasibility of private voting within the governance model. It was determined that private voting is incompatible with a delegation-based liquid democracy, as delegators must have the capacity to publicly verify votes to maintain trust and dynamically reallocate their delegation. The team concluded that rather than modifying the transparency of the architecture, the ecosystem must actively address the pressuring behavior of hostile actors to protect representative autonomy, expressing that this topic requires further consideration.
- **Decentralized Parameter Polls and Community Engagement:** The team agreed to participate in an active technical poll launched by a community-led working group on block reward distribution parameters. To encourage broader ecosystem engagement amid low participation rates, CF might participate to signal support for decentralized parameter research and utilize public channels to advertise the poll, highlighting the value of grassroots technical contributions to protocol optimization.
