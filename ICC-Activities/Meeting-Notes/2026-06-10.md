## Meeting Recap

The meeting addressed six pending governance actions, focusing on strategic alignments and technical evaluations of expiring proposals. The team finalized recommendations regarding the Scalus and Decentralized Open Source Program Office (OSPO) proposals. A wallet business transition proposal from Eternl was evaluated for supportive positioning. Strategic support was given to the Constitutional Committee (CC) Minimum Size parameter change to protect the network from operational block. The team also reviewed the Rare Evo event proposal, addressing concerns over non-essential line items and the transition of marketing expenditures to decentralized models. Finally, the team reviewed the upcoming Intersect budget proposals and discussed adjustments to the compilation of rationales for high-volume submissions.
