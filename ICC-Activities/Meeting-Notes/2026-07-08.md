## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting addressed 10 active governance actions currently under evaluation, focusing on the rapid consumption of the Net Change Limit (NCL) and its implications for pending treasury withdrawals. The team considered a strategy to draft a standardized "No" vote rationale template to be used once the NCL is fully exhausted, advising projects to resubmit under a future limit. The team also considered the issue of how the community can move towards structured budget planning to most efficiently use the NCL in the future. Assessments were discussed for the Alchemy / Sundial Bitcoin Treasury proposal, resubmissions of Blockfrost, Scalus, and the Decentralized OSPO proposal, as well as resubmissions from the Interset-led 2026 budget process (including Daedalus, Anvil Ticketing Platform, Cardano Builder DAO, and Global Order Book). Additionally, the team analyzed timing conflicts between hard fork parameter changes and treasury withdrawal enactments, agreeing to expedite voting for key proposals to prevent expiration drops.
