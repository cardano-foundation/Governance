## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting focused on active proposal performance, voting strategy, and candidate evaluations for the ongoing Constitutional Committee (CC) election. The team reviewed pending governance actions, including the status of Intersect budget proposals and resubmission approaches. In preparation for filling four open Constitutional Committee seats, the team analyzed candidate submissions and discussed how the operational structure of the CC could improve in the future to become more efficient for its members and the community at large.
