## Meeting Recap

The meeting evaluated 10 active governance actions requiring team review, focusing on projected Net Change Limit (NCL) headroom shortages and strategic treasury management. Deliberations covered resubmitted Intersect budget proposals (Cardano Builder DAO, ticketing platform, and Daedalus maintenance) as well as the Alchemy proposal. Strategic discussions examined the political and logical trade-offs surrounding the AlphaGrowth proposal, requesting 120 million ada, and proposed NCL increases. Additionally, the team discussed the utility of a unified infrastructure roadmap for future budget cycles and structured a technical verification plan regarding exchange stack dependencies for the Blockfrost non-profit transformation proposal.

## Details

- **Governance Status and NCL Headroom Analysis:** The team conducted a comprehensive review of the NCL, currently capped at 350 million ada. As of July 22nd 2026, approximately 302 million ada has been allocated across approved governance actions. Following the upcoming epoch boundary, six enacted treasury withdrawals will leave roughly 47 million ada in active NCL. With nearly 12 pending treasury actions requesting a collective 225 million ada, projections indicate that after the August 2nd epoch boundary, remaining NCL capacity will drop to approximately 14 million ada. Consequently, the team analyzed the constraints facing high-budget actions under current constitutional spending limits.
- **Evaluation of July 28th Governance Expirations:** The team reviewed 5 governance actions approaching their expiration date on July 28th. The discussion distinguished standalone submissions without broader ecosystem alignment, such as the Alchemy proposal, from proposals originating from the Intersect budget process, specifically Cardano Builder DAO, the Anvil ticketing platform, and Daedalus wallet maintenance. The team noted that for the Intersect proposals, the underlying technical scope and delivery terms remain unchanged from earlier evaluations, framing the discussion around technical continuity and prior review rationale.
- **Holistic Spending and NCL Expansion Debate:** A strategic discussion was held regarding the trade off between supporting viable proposals and operating within severe NCL constraints. The team reviewed a proposal seeking to raise the NCL to 500 million ada alongside individual requests like AlphaGrowth. The team discussed the inconsistencies of considering high-budget growth initiatives while evaluating NCL ceiling adjustments without broader budget planning. The discussion highlighted the risks of arbitrary, reactive ceiling increases in favor of structured budget allocation, emphasizing that proposals exceeding realistic treasury headroom must be evaluated against overall network priorities rather than processed on a first-come, first-served basis.
- **Future NCL Planning and Allocation:** The team outlined a plan to transition toward a structured annual NCL review process for 2027 for the CF’s DRep voting. Recognizing the lack of a centralized infrastructure roadmap, the advisory team is considering an internal polling process across engineering and operational departments. This survey will identify and prioritize the ecosystem's top core technical requirements for the next several years, establishing an objective benchmark to guide future governance evaluation and resource allocation.
- **Blockfrost Infrastructure Transformation and Technical Verification:** The team evaluated the governance action proposing the transformation of Blockfrost into a non-profit entity. While public discourse and external endorsements have generated significant attention, the team examined claims characterizing the service as an irreplaceable public dependency for major exchanges, noting that institutional trading venues typically operate proprietary nodes and dedicated indexing tooling. The team outlined a technical verification plan with internal infrastructure leads to assess actual exchange integration dependencies and evaluate alternative open-source tooling options before finalizing their rationale.
