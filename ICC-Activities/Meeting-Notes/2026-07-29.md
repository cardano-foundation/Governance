## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting reviewed recent governance action outcomes following the epoch boundary, addressed the Net Change Limit (NCL) deficit, and established an aligned policy regarding the separation of proposal merit evaluation from constitutional NCL limits. The team reached a positive voting consensus on resubmitted proposals originating from the Intersect budget process (Global Order Book) and the Scalus resubmission. Deliberations continued regarding the proposal to raise the NCL ceiling to 500 million ada and its interplay with the AlphaGrowth proposal. Finally, the team conducted technical and business assessments for the Bifrost Bitcoin-Cardano bridge proposal, deferring a final recommendation to allow for further review prior to the upcoming epoch deadline.
