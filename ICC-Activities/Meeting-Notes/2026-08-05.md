## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting addressed recent governance action outcomes following the expiration of several high-profile initiatives, reviewed the official Net Change Limit (NCL) increase to 500 million ada, and the resubmitted Decentralized Open Source Program proposal, which continues to present structural governance concerns due to its sole administrator framework. Extensive deliberations centered on the AlphaGrowth proposal requesting 120 million ada, weighing its substantial fee structure, strategic clarity, and oversight neutrality against ecosystem growth considerations, leading to a decision to await further operational insights before concluding an internal recommendation.

Discussions also covered protocol-level adjustments and constitutional committee continuity, beginning with parameter changes submitted by the Technical Steering Committee to lower the minimum pool cost to 75 ada and expand Plutus transaction execution memory limits. The team evaluated an info action regarding hard fork naming. Finally, the team evaluated the update to the Constitutional Committee composition to ensure the system remains operational, while weighing procedural mechanisms to address long-term member alignment and voting consistency.
