## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting addressed current on-chain voting participation and threshold dynamics across active governance actions, focusing on the Update Constitutional Committee 2026 and Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada + increase Plutus Memory Limits (Part 2). To ensure operational continuity given a DRep participation rate near 20% and SPO approval below the 51% threshold, the team evaluated several strategic interventions. These included coordinating closely with Intersect, launching targeted communications for stake pool operators, and conducting outreach with exchanges regarding auto-abstention mechanisms of their stake pools. Additionally, members discussed the newly submitted Governance Incentives Framework 2026 proposal, weighing concerns around administrative oversight, disclosure of previous funding, and thematic overlap with existing research efforts of IOR, while discussing whether an experimental pilot model with concrete hypotheses would offer a more suitable alternative.
