## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting addressed active governance actions, noting participation levels for both the minimum pool cost parameter change and the OpenZeppelin treasury withdrawal proposal. The team evaluated the treasury request against existing tooling overlap, particularly with ongoing developer experience initiatives funded earlier in 2026, while weighing the institutional credibility and enterprise visibility introduced by OpenZeppelin's acquisition by S&P Global. The discussion centered on whether public treasury allocation is justified for external brand acquisition and standardized frameworks, balancing long-term developer onboarding value against the efficiency of existing ecosystem repositories.

Extensive deliberations focused on the info action regarding the stake pool target parameter, which aims to gather sentiment on raising stakePoolTargetNum (k) from 500 to 1,000. The team analyzed the shifting economic incentives facing stake pool operators, examining how a lower saturation ceiling impacts multi-pool operators, institutional custodians like Blockdaemon, and single community pools with broad delegator bases. Advisors scrutinized reward distribution mechanics, noting that oversaturated pool rewards face significant treasury taxation, and debated whether adjusting k meaningfully improves decentralization or merely shifts delegation friction onto end users.
