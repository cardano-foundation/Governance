## Meeting Recap

The weekly meeting addressed active on-chain actions and protocol parameter changes, focusing on the OpenZeppelin treasury withdrawal proposal and the info action regarding the stake pool target parameter k. The OpenZeppelin proposal lags considerably behind required ratification thresholds and overlaps with wider developer experience efforts. Extensive debate centered on the info action proposing to double stakePoolTargetNum (k) from 500 to 1,000, with advisors examining long-term pool profitability, hardware operational costs, and the fixed-reserve economics facing the network over a ten-year horizon. The team also weighed the strategic alignment between the Foundation’s DRep and SPO roles, deliberating whether consistency in voting provides clearer guidance to the community than split positions.
